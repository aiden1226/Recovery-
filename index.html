Taking care of yourself

General information for when you're feeling sick.

Rest and stay comfortable

Get enough rest, drink fluids regularly, and let a parent or trusted adult know when you're feeling unwell.

When to get medical advice

Tell a trusted adult and contact a healthcare professional if symptoms are worsening, aren't improving, or you're concerned about a fever, pain or dehydration.

Emergency symptoms

Get emergency help for serious breathing difficulties, severe chest pain, confusion, fainting, or difficulty waking up. Call 911 in the United States.

Your sickness rating does not determine whether you need medical attention. This app is a personal tracker, not a diagnostic or emergency service.

Data & privacy

Your entries and imported sleep records are stored locally in this browser without encryption. Avoid entering sensitive information on shared devices. Clearing browser data may erase your records.

Apple Health integration uses read-only sleep exports through Apple Shortcuts. This website does not upload your recovery or sleep records to a server.

Clipboard access occurs only when you press Paste from clipboard. Sleep data may remain on your clipboard after importing.

Clearing imported sleep does not delete anything from Apple Health or revoke shortcut permissions. Review the shortcut's actions before granting access.

Apple Health

Connect your sleep data

1
Install Sick Sleep Sync

Add the shortcut to your iPhone. Review its actions before granting permission.

2
Check the shortcut

Make sure it is named exactly Sick Sleep Sync. Its final action should be Copy to Clipboard. Remove URL and Open URLs actions if present.

3
Sync your sleep records

Run the shortcut and allow read access to Sleep when requested. Wait until it finishes copying.

4
Import your sleep

Return to Recovery. Tap Import sleep data, paste the copied records, and press Import.

Import sleep data

Paste the output from Sick Sleep Sync. JSON is not required.
