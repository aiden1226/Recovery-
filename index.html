Privacy & safety

Your records are stored locally in this browser without encryption. Avoid storing private health information on shared devices. Clearing browser storage may remove your records.

Apple Health access is read-only through your installed shortcut. This website does not upload your sleep records to a server. Clearing imported sleep does not delete records from Apple Health or revoke the shortcut's Health permissions.

This website does not diagnose illnesses or replace medical care. If you're feeling seriously unwell, tell a trusted adult or seek medical help. For emergencies, call 911 in the United States.

Connect Apple Health

Install Sick Sleep Sync to export your sleep records using Apple Shortcuts.

1. Install the shortcut.

2. Make sure its name is exactly Sick Sleep Sync.

3. Run the shortcut and grant read-only Sleep permission when requested.

4. Return here and import the copied sleep data.
